International Clinical DR Grading Scale

Our AI model is mapped strictly to the recognized 5-tier ICDR criteria for ophthalmic screening.

GRADE 0

No Apparent DR

No retinal microaneurysms, hemorrhages, or exudates present. Normal vascular appearance.

Action: Routine annual follow-up.
GRADE 1

Mild NPDR

Microaneurysms only. Small outpouchings of capillary walls visible as tiny red dots.

Action: Re-evaluate in 6–12 months.
GRADE 2

Moderate NPDR

More than microaneurysms, but less than severe: dot/blot hemorrhages, hard yellow exudates, cotton-wool spots.

Action: Refer for ophthalmology review.
GRADE 3

Severe NPDR

>20 intraretinal hemorrhages in each of 4 quadrants, definite venous beading in 2+ quadrants, or IRMA in 1+ quadrant.

Action: Prompt retina specialist consultation.
GRADE 4

Proliferative DR

Neovascularization of the disc/retina (NVD/NVE), and/or vitreous/preretinal hemorrhage.

Action: Urgent intervention (laser/anti-VEGF).
